    What goes into the price of an AI project

    Four factors account for most of the difference between one quote and another. Knowing them makes the estimate above easier to read, and makes any quote you receive easier to question.

    Scope before software

    The biggest swing in an AI budget is not the model, it is how much of your business the system has to understand. An assistant that answers ten common questions and one that handles a full order lifecycle sit far apart in price.

    Your data decides the effort

    Clean, well-structured data lets a team move straight to building. Scattered records, or data that has to be gathered and labelled first, can add a third to the cost before a single feature is written.

    Integrations add up quietly

    Every system the AI has to read from or write to — CRM, ERP, billing, messaging — brings its own permissions, formats and failure cases. Two integrations is routine, eight is a project of its own.

    Running costs continue after launch

    Models drift, prompts need tuning and usage grows. Budget for ongoing support, roughly a sixth of the build cost per year, so the system keeps earning its place.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does custom AI development cost?

    Most custom AI projects land between USD 15,000 and USD 120,000. A focused chatbot or a proof of concept sits at the lower end, while a computer vision system or an enterprise AI platform with custom model training sits at the upper end. The calculator above gives you a range based on your own scope.

    What makes one AI project cost more than another?

    Four things move the number most: how complex the workflows are, whether you can use a ready-made model or need one trained on your data, the state of that data, and how many systems the AI has to connect to. Compliance requirements and on-premise hosting add to it as well.

    How long does it take to build an AI solution?

    A small pilot can go live in six to eight weeks. A production system with several workflows and integrations usually runs three to six months. Custom model training and regulated industries extend that further, mostly because of data preparation and approvals.

    Can we start smaller to prove the value first?

    Yes, and it is usually the better route. A focused pilot on one workflow shows you the real accuracy and the real savings on your own data, and gives you a firm basis for scoping the full build.

    Is this estimate the final price?

    No. It is a planning range built from typical projects. A firm quote follows a short discovery call where we go through your workflows, data and systems, and it is fixed in writing before any work starts.
